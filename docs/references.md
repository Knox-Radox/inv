# References

## The two client references

Watched at 430×932. The `/demo/` URLs are product pages; the live templates are
`template-bellagio.thedigitalyes.com` and `tdy-excellence-template.thedigitalyes.com`.

**What they get right — pacing, and material.** Bellagio's cover is an embroidered
linen wrapper tied with a silk ribbon; it parts as two panels over roughly 3.5
seconds, slow enough that you watch rather than wait. Maison Dorée's wax seal is
the quality bar for material: irregular pooled edge, specular catch at ten
o'clock, a genuinely debossed monogram. Both prove that stillness plus one long
move beats many short ones.

**What they get wrong.** The ornament is photographed, not drawn. Measured
payload: Bellagio 37.7 MB, Maison Dorée 47.9 MB, of which 33 MB is video. On
congested LTE that is not an invitation, it is a wait. Maison Dorée's cover is a
`fixed inset-0` div over `body{overflow:hidden}` whose only exit is a video that
must play — when it doesn't, the guest is locked out with no skip, no label, no
fallback. Its countdown has already expired and reads `00 DAYS 00 HOURS 00
MINUTES`. An untranslated key, `PROGRAM.CRUISESUBDESC`, is live on the page.
Bellagio prints "View on Map" twice and overflows tracked caps past the right
edge at 430px.

**The room they leave.** Neither attempts a *drawn* line, so their embroidery
cannot draw on, respond, scale, or answer `prefers-reduced-motion`. Neither has a
structural idea: every section is centred script over a picture, nine times over.
And neither degrades — both are all-or-nothing.

## Line study — public-domain engravings

*Hortus Malabaricus* tab. 86 (Van Rheede, 1678–93), and the Wellcome plates of
*Jasminum sambac* and *Mangifera indica*. Studied, not collected.

What a burin does that a vector doesn't: the stroke tapers to nothing at a
growing tip; a single leaf's contour is a hairline on its lit edge and three
times heavier on its shadowed one; secondary veins stop short of the margin; the
line swells at every node where two strokes meet; highlights are reserved paper,
never painted on. Nothing is uniform, and nothing is mirrored.
